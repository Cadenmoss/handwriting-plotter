Handwriting capture

a

Write inside the box with a stylus, finger or mouse. Sit letters on the solid baseline; lowercase letters like x should reach the dashed line. Tails (g, y, p) go below the baseline. Write naturally and vary each repeat a little.

  1. Capture all glyphs (the chips turn green; click any chip to jump to it).
  2. Download glyphs.json and put it in the project folder.
  3. Run write.py to turn text into pen strokes.
